Topic Of Peace
Peace is one of the most important parts of our world and is essential for a harmonious society. Many countries have experienced the devastating effects of conflict and war, highlighting the critical need for peaceful resolution of disputes. Peace is not just the absence of war and violence and crime, but a state of harmony not only betweeen people but between nations on a wider scale. To acheive peace, working together is the only strategy to allow for the betterment of society. Once peace is acheived on the wider scale, it can lead to others on the world to become more aware and further try to maintain peace.
