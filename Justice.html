The Topic of Justice
Justice is one of the three aspects of this topic and it helps to understand the importance of maintaining fairness and finding ways to end/decrease inequality in the world. Many people around the globe face challenges related to justice, inclduing places such as the United States of America, where racial prejudice and discrimination have been major issues for decades. This issue of justice has been a major problem in the world and has lead to a lot of war and violence in the world. Children and adults alike face the consequences of this injustice, which manifests in various forms of discrimination and unequal treatment under the law. In order to address the goal that the world has in mind we must stand against injustice and properly judge persons.
